Heliophysics Mission

Aditya-L1: Unlocking Solar Dynamics

Stationed at Sun-Earth Lagrange Point L1, 1.5 million kilometers from Earth.

Halo Orbit Advantage

At L1, the gravitational pulls of Earth and the Sun balance with orbital centripetal force. This provides an uninterrupted view of the Sun without planetary occultation or eclipses.

Primary Payloads

VELC: Visible Emission Line Coronagraph observing coronal mass ejections.
SUIT: Solar Ultraviolet Imaging Telescope taking full-disk UV images of the photosphere and chromosphere.

Launched Sept 2, 2023 • Stationed at Halo Orbit Jan 6, 2024 Slide 5 of 10
Lunar Exploration

Chandrayaan-3: Shiv Shakti Point Science

In-situ scientific experiments on lunar soil composition and thermal behavior.

🧪

LIBS & APXS Payloads

Pragyan rover confirmed the presence of Sulphur (S), Aluminum, Calcium, Iron, and Titanium in lunar regolith.

🌡️

ChaSTE Thermal Probe

Discovered high thermal gradient: 50°C on surface dropping to -10°C just 8 cm beneath the topsoil.

🦘

Hop Experiment

Vikram lander fired engines to hop 40 cm and safely reland, proving technology for future sample return missions.

Historic soft-landing August 23, 2023 • National Space Day Slide 6 of 10
Human Spaceflight

Gaganyaan: Crew Escape & Life Support

Demonstrating indigenous capabilities to launch humans to 400 km LEO and return safely.

Orbital Module System

Consists of the Crew Module (CM) housing 3 astronauts with dual-redundant Environmental Control and Life Support Systems (ECLSS), and the unpressurized Service Module (SM).

Test Vehicle (TV-D1) Flight

Successfully demonstrated in-flight abort simulation at Mach 1.2, jettisoning the Crew Module away from the launch vehicle with parachute recovery in the Bay of Bengal.
