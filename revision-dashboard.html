Orbital Mechanics 💡 Question
What is the formula for Earth's escape velocity?
Tap card to reveal formula & derivation
Card 1 of 6 Difficulty: Medium
Solution ✅ Answer
$$v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}} \approx 11.2\text{ km/s}$$

At this minimum speed, an object escapes Earth's gravitational field without further propulsion.
